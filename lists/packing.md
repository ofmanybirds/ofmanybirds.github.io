This is a list of things you're supposed to pack for various kinds of travel. Each list also contains all of the content of the prior lists -- you're always supposed to pack water, for example.  

##### Extremely light.  
This is about those situations where you're just going outside for a little bit of time.   

Water.  
Keys.  
Wallet.  
Identity documents.  
Basic hygiene stuff if you need that.  
Medication. (I recommend keeping your meds in your wallet. Yes, even the once-a-day stuff that you don't think you'll need.)  
The appropriate amount of money.  

##### The one where you're staying somewhere for a single day.  

Some food. 
Deodorant.  
Maybe a change of underwear, you probably don't need a change of clothes overall but feel free to bring that also.
