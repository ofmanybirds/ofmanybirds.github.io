We played some Terraria Calamity (Expert + Revengeance), and are doing Infernum now. We have some thoughts on the two games (and, yes, Calamity is a separate game).  

The bad (general): 
You have to resummon minions after dying. Just, why. You want the same ones approximately always, it just increases latency.  
Potion farming sucks. They should just be infinite by default.    
Everything is so slow. Seriously, how do people play this game without quality of life mods?  
Quite a few bosses are constantly off-screen, and it makes stuff very annoying.  
How much you can see is tied to display resolution for some eldritch reason.  
Biome-based enraging is a bad mechanic. It does not actually prevent you from building a larger arena, it just forces you to do so with actuators or whatever. Incredibly inconvenient, for no practical benefit.  
Golem sucks. I just want to be able to make arenas. Arenas where visibility is nice. In broad daylight, preferably. And I don't have to use weapons that go through walls to do anything.  
Visual clarity in the game is trash. Also, so little light. Shine and Night Owl potions are basically required, and the optimal strategy while caving without them involves staring at a completely dark screen for long periods of time. Just, why.  

The bad (Calamity): 
The lategame weapons -- see The Old Reaper -- produce so much visual clutter. I had to just turn it off.   
A lot of grind. And the chained trees were just not fun, we had all of the items but just had to click through menus so many times to get the gear.   
The Codebreaker and the power cells force you to wait for two and a half hours or grind or something, you just need to have set some up in the early game. Baffling design choice.

Boss thoughts (Revengeance):  
Most things took one or two attempts, which is interesting -- we'd only played Classic before, and that was years ago. Not entirely sure what the skill transfer is from.  
We did Brain of Cthulhu and Perforators at once (killed a cyst by accident), still somehow managed it first try. Was fun.  
Brimstone Elemental is red projectiles on a red background. Hell.  
Providence and Guardians were really fun -- I like fights where I can see the projectiles and just dodge them. 
Devourer of Gods, I really liked the rhythm. 
Supreme Calamitas, absolutely the best fight in the mod, loved it.  
Exo Mechs -- very cluttered fight. Literally just wants an ungodly amount of vertical space. I can't figure out what else is happening.  
Old Duke is a visual mess. Seriously, why. 

Boss thoughts (Infernum): 
Deerclops is very glitchy. Supposedly this is a thing in vanilla too, but I only fought her in Infernum. Kept phasing through the floor. 
Skeletron is incredible and I love the boss a lot.  
Slime God is a really neat fight, it's just very calm dodging over long periods of time and I liked that. 
Wall of Flesh is really fun, I like the rhythm. 
Dreadnautilus is very fast and basically impossible to aim at. And it's constantly off-screen.  
Cryogen is white projectiles on a white-blue background. Treachery. Also, why does it enrage? I did not want to build an arena for it, sucks.
