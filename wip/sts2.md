We've been playing some Slay the Spire 2 recently, and it's been really fun. Here's some very loose thoughts on the whole thing.  

#### A list of the scaling options that exist on each character. 

##### Ironclad.   
At Common, Armaments. And I suppose True Grit. The Vulnerable synergy space, being Tremble, Molten Fist, and Thunderlap, are kind of scaling.   
At Uncommon, Ashen Strike, Bully, Burning Pact, Dismantle, Dominate, Feel No Pain, Fight Me!, Inflame, Inferno, Juggling, Rampage, Rupture, Second Wind, Stampede, Stone Armor, Vicious.   
At Rare, Aggression, Barricade, Brand, Colossus, Crimson Mantle, Cruelty, Dark Embrace, Demon Form, Fiend Fire, Hellraiser, Juggernaut, Pact's End, Primal Force, Pyre, Stoke, Tear Asunder, Thrash, Unmovable.   

Notes: Common scaling options are really really bad. The Rares are almost all scaling, and the Uncommons are pretty decent. Fighting at least some Elites seems essential to beating bosses.  

##### Silent.   
At Common, Snakebite, Poisoned Stab, Deadly Poison.   
At Uncommon, Accuracy, Bouncing Flask, Bubble Bubble, Blur, Footwork, Hand Trick, Haze, Infinite Blades, Noxious Fumes, Mirage, Outbreak, Phantom Blades, Speedster, Up My Sleeve, Well-Laid Plans.  
At Rare, Abrasive, Accelerant, Afterimage, Envenom, Knife Trap, Malaise, Master Planner, Murder, Nightmare, Serpent Form, Tools of the Trade, Tracking.  

Notes: Really good scaling at Common -- you don't *need* to take Elites to beat bosses, so it's sensible to prioritise that less and not put as many shitty damage commons in to get a leaner deck overall.  

##### Regent.   
At Common, BEGONE!, Spoils of Battle, Wrought in War, Refine Blade.   
At Uncommon, Black Hole, Bulwark, CHARGE!!, Child of the Stars, Conqueror, Furnace, Kingly Kick, Kingly Punch, Orbit, Pale Blue Dot, Parry, Pillar of Creation, Resonance, Royal Gamble, Spectrum shift, Summon Forth, Supermassive.   
At Rare, Arsenal, Beat into Shape, Big Bang, Bombardment, Foregone Conclusion, Genesis, GUARDS!!!, Monarch's Gaze, Neutron Aegis, Seeking Edge, Sword Sage, The Smith, Tyranny, Void Form.  

Notes: Some elites seem really nice, there's a decent density of good Uncommons. Rares, not so much.
