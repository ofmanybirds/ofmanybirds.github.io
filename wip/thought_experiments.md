This post is about some thought experiments which are not very good. Their descriptions are taken from Wikipedia.  

1. Mary's room.  

2. p-zombies.   

3. Chinese room.  

4. China brain.

5. Twin Earth.

6. Experience machine.

Premise 1: If experiencing as much pleasure as we can is all that matters to us, then if we will experience more pleasure by doing x than by doing y, we have no reason to do y rather than x.
Premise 2: We will experience more pleasure if we plug into the experience machine than if we do not plug into the experience machine.
Conclusion 1: If experiencing as much pleasure as we can is all that matters to us, then we have no reason not to plug into the experience machine. (P1&P2)
Premise 3: We have reason not to plug into the experience machine.
Conclusion 2: Experiencing as much pleasure as we can is not all that matters to us.

You can care about other people. Ought to, even. This gives classical utilitarians very valid reasons to not want to be plugged into the experience machine. 
In a world where one exists, you want to make sure that the amount of time for which such a machine is
running is as close to unbounded as possible, and you can't meaningfully work on the problem while already plugged in.  
You also can't have that high a level of confidence that the experience machine would even work, and that fundamentally limits the expected value. Even in a world where what you think might be an experience machine exists,
immediately plugging oneself into it is nowhere close to guaranteed to be correct.   
The secondary aspect is that hedonism is not only caring about pleasure, only believing that one ought to do so. We can care about other things, we just think we're incorrect about this.  
The tertiary aspect is that plugging into the experience machine makes one less capable of reacting to the outside world, and means the simulation might collapse at any moment due to factors outside of one's control. There are real risks,
ones not shared by the "taking a pill that makes one permanently happy without otherwise impacting them" variation. I believe that one to be a superior representation of hedonistic beliefs.
