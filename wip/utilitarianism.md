0 -- Basic personal thoughts.  

Utilitarianism is a theory we use as a criterion of rightness. We conceptualise the criterion as a well-ordering of all reachable states of reality by goodness, with the best one being the state one ought to reach. Reaching the highest-goodness state is the correct thing to do, and doing anything less than that is incorrect. It is a well-ordering, though, so some states are better than others, and actions can be more or less good.  

1 -- The demandingness objection.    

Quite a lot of people argue that utilitarianism is too demanding, with the theory leaving no room for actions that are permissible but not required.  
Our view is that if a theory of moral value just tells you to keep doing what you're already doing, it might not actually be a theory. We value those because they make novel predictions which are accurate, as it happens.
