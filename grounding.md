Some grounding exercises we use.  

The five senses technique is good. Name 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste. For us, it helps to name them in multiple languages, conjugate them a bit. Once we're drawing connections, stuff's usually gotten a lot better, and it's not that harder if we're doing stuff with language.  
Box breathing can be helpful. Any consistent breathing pattern does it, really.  
We find typing helpful, or just imagining ourselves typing. Song lyrics are common.  
Splashing water in our face just, works sometimes.
