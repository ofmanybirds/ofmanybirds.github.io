In our experience, there are at least two kinds of trust.  

The first is trust in competence. Do you believe that a given person will be able to accomplish their goals? Do you at least believe they'll give it an honest attempt?  
The second is trust in alignment. Do you believe their goals are aligned with yours? Do you believe they value the same things you value?  

If you trust someone's neither competence nor alignment, they are largely unpredictable to you.
